DAO Launch Starter Kit

Operational checklist to register voters, quorum, and DAO roles.

1. Pre-Launch Checklist

Define Membership Criteria

Token gating (ERC-20/SPL) vs. NFT access vs. Whitelist.

Set Governance Parameters

Quorum (e.g., 4%), Voting Period (e.g., 3 days), Timelock (e.g., 24h).

Select Tooling Stack

Realms (Solana) / Snapshot (Off-chain) / Tally (On-chain).

2. Role Definitions

Council / Multisig

Executes decisions passed by the DAO. Holds the keys to the treasury but cannot act unilaterally.

Token Holders

Propose and vote on initiatives. Their power is proportional to their stake.

Delegates

Active community members who vote on behalf of passive holders to ensure quorum.

3. Launch Timeline

  1. 1

    Soft Launch (Week 1)

    Deploy contracts to devnet. Test voting flow with core team.

  2. 2

    Genesis Proposal (Week 2)

    First on-chain vote to ratify the DAO constitution.

  3. 3

    Treasury Handover (Week 4)

    Transfer project funds to the DAO multisig.
