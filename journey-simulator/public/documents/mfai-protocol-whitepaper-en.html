Strategic vision, economic primitives, and MFAI deployment roadmaps.

Abstract

Money Factory AI (MFAI) introduces a paradigm shift in decentralized finance by integrating autonomous AI agents directly into the protocol layer. By automating complex decision-making processes—from yield farming strategies to DAO governance—MFAI democratizes access to sophisticated financial tools and maximizes capital efficiency for all participants.

1. Introduction

The complexity of the Web3 ecosystem has become a barrier to entry. Users are overwhelmed by the number of chains, protocols, and governance proposals. MFAI solves this by deploying "Zyno" agents—personal AI financial architects that understand user goals and execute strategies autonomously.
