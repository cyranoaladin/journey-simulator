Money Factory AI Litepaper

This excerpt outlines the mission, architecture, and economic design of Money Factory AI. The full litepaper contains extended compliance analysis, formal proofs, and technical appendices.

1 · Mission & Thesis

Money Factory AI orchestrates venture creation on Solana through the Cognitive Activation Protocol™. We combine individualized AEPO journeys with cohort-scale AECO orchestration to activate teams, capital, and liquidity in weeks instead of quarters.

  • Activation: transform async learning into staged proof-of-skill credentials anchored on-chain.
  • Acceleration: deploy the Zyno multi-agent mesh to compress diligence, token design, and go-to-market loops.
  • Capitalization: channel qualified projects into the Collaterize launchpad for programmable fundraising.

AEPO Completion

82% of builders complete Phase 2 missions within 18 days. Proof-of-Skill NFTs certify progress.

Capital Velocity

Collaterize simulations show a 28% reduction in time-to-liquidity once a project enters the launch queue.

Agent Efficiency

17 specialized AI agents handle 63% of diligence tasks, freeing mentors for strategic interventions.

2 · Architecture Overview

  • Journey Layer: React/Vite client with Zustand state, zk-enabled proof issuance, and wallet orchestration.
  • Orchestration Layer: Node/Express backend with Mongo persistence, agent routing, and RAG knowledge vault.
  • Agent Fabric: Zyno agents covering venture, technical, compliance, and community functions with AEPO telemetry.
  • Capital Layer: Collaterize integration for bonding curves, liquidity bootstrapping, and treasury governance.

3 · Economic Design

$MFAI aligns builders, contributors, and capital providers.

  • Utility: staking unlocks premium missions, cross-cohort governance, and fee rebates on launch events.
  • Flows: protocol revenues (launch fees, AI agent marketplace, licensing) funnel into buy-and-make reserves.
  • Stability: bonding curve parameters adapt based on AECO health scores and Collaterize liquidity depth.
