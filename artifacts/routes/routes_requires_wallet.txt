# Routes Requiring Wallet Connection

## Frontend (Wallet-Protected Layout)
/journeys (real journeys, not demo)
/journeys/:journeyId
/journeys/completed
/dao
/debug/mint

## Backend (Wallet/Web3 operations)
/api/solana/connect
/api/solana/verify
/api/solana/transaction/*
/api/dao/vote/*
/api/dao/stake/*

Note: In connect-only mode, wallet connect is permitted but on-chain transactions (mint, stake, vote) are blocked/simulated.
