/dashboard
/playground
/resources
/support
/zyno
/guide
/journeys
/journeys/:journeyId
/journeys/completed
/dao

## Backend (Require JWT)
All routes under:
- /api/journey/*
- /api/agents/*
- /api/user/*
- /api/orchestration/*
- /api/zyno/*
- /api/rag/*
- /api/analytics/*
- /api/dao/* (most endpoints)
- /api/solana/* (most endpoints)

Exceptions (public):
- /health
- /auth/login
- /auth/register
- /auth/nonce
- /demo/*
